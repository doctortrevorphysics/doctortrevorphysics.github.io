Gravitational Potential Energy

Learning Objectives

  • To use the equation for gravitational potential energy to determine the change in gravitational potential energy, mass, initial height, final height and/or change in height when given appropriate information.
  • To define the term potential energy.
  • To use energy considerations including kinetic and potential energy to solve problems.

What is Potential Energy?

Potential energy is stored energy that depends upon the relative position of various parts of a system. When we lift an object against Earth's gravity, we are transferring and storing energy into the object-Earth system, which we call gravitational potential energy.

The Gravitational Potential Energy Equation

The change in an object's gravitational potential energy can be determined by multiplying its mass, the acceleration due to gravity, and its vertical displacement.

Δ U = m g Δ h

Where:

  • ΔU = Change in gravitational potential energy (Joules, J)
  • m = mass (kilograms, kg)
  • g = magnitude of acceleration due to gravity (9.80 m/s2)
  • Δh = vertical displacement or change in height (meters, m)

Caution: Reference Frames and Mass vs. Weight

Height is always relative! It is crucial to define a reference level (like setting the floor as h=0) before starting your problem. Additionally, be careful not to confuse mass (measured in kg) with weight (measured in Newtons). The gravitational potential energy equation requires you to use the mass of the object.

Concept: Path Independence

The change in gravitational potential energy depends only on the vertical change in height, not the path taken to get there. For example, lifting a box straight up vertically requires the exact same change in energy as pushing it up a frictionless ramp to the same final height!

Sample Problem: Moving a Heavy Box

A warehouse worker lifts a heavy box with a mass of 15.0 kg from the floor to the top shelf, which is 2.50 m above the floor. Assuming the floor is the reference level of zero, what is the change in the gravitational potential energy of the box?

Knowns:

  • m=15.0 kg
  • g=+9.80 m/s2
  • Δh=+2.50 m

Unknowns:

  • ΔU=? J

Starting Equation:

Δ U = m g Δ h

Algebraic Solution:

We are trying to solve for the change in gravitational potential energy. Looking at our starting equation, the variable of interest (ΔU) is already isolated on the left side. Therefore, no algebraic manipulation is necessary for this problem!

Isolated Equation:

Δ U = m g Δ h

Substitution:

Δ U = ( 15.0  kg ) ( + 9.80  m/ s 2 ) ( + 2.50  m )

Unrounded Final Answer:

Δ U = + 367.500  J

Final Answer (Adjusted for Significant Digits):

We review the given values in our problem: the mass (15.0 kg), the acceleration due to gravity (9.80 m/s²), and the vertical displacement (2.50 m) all contain exactly three significant digits. Following the rules for multiplication, our final answer must also be rounded to three significant digits.

Δ U = + 368  J

Lesson Review

  • Potential energy is energy based on an object's position within a system.
  • The equation to find the change in gravitational potential energy is ΔU=mgΔh.
  • Always define a reference point where height (and thus potential energy) is considered to be zero.
  • The change in energy depends only on the vertical displacement, not the route taken to achieve that displacement.
