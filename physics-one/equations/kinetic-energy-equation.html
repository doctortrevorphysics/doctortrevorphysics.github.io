Kinetic Energy

Learning Objectives

  • To state the equation for the kinetic energy of an object.
  • To use the equation for kinetic energy to determine the kinetic energy, mass, and/or velocity of an object when given appropriate information.
  • To describe the effects on kinetic energy when changing the mass or speed of an object.

The Kinetic Energy Equation

Kinetic energy is the energy of motion. Any object that has mass and is moving possesses kinetic energy. The fundamental equation for kinetic energy is defined as:

K = 1 2 m v 2

Variable Definitions:

  • K = Kinetic Energy (measured in Joules, J)
  • m = Mass of the object (measured in kilograms, kg)
  • v = Velocity of the object (measured in meters per second, m/s)

Caution: Common Mistakes!

When using the kinetic energy equation, keep two things in mind:

  • Only the velocity is squared! Make sure you square the velocity before multiplying by the mass and one-half.
  • Watch your units! Mass must always be in kilograms (kg) to result in Joules. If you are given grams (g), you must convert to kg first.

Concept Box: The Power of Speed

Because the velocity term is squared in our equation (v2), speed has a massive impact on kinetic energy. If you double the mass of an object, you double its kinetic energy. However, if you double the speed of an object, its kinetic energy quadruples (22=4)!

Sample Calculation: A Baseball Pitch

A baseball with a mass of 0.145 kg is thrown by a pitcher. If the baseball possesses 109 Joules of kinetic energy, what is the velocity of the pitch?

Knowns:

  • m = 0.145 kg
  • K = 109 J

Unknowns:

  • v = ? m/s

Starting Equation:

K = 1 2 m v 2

To solve for velocity, we must first isolate v. We begin by multiplying both sides by 2 to remove the fraction:

2 K = m v 2

Next, we divide both sides by the mass (m):

2 K m = v 2

Finally, we take the square root of both sides to isolate the velocity:

Isolated Equation:

v = 2 K m

Substitution:

Now we substitute our known values into the isolated equation:

v = 2 ( 109  J ) ( 0.145  kg )

Unrounded Final Answer:

v = + 38.7743  m/s

Final Answer:

Because both of our known values (109 J and 0.145 kg) were provided with three significant digits, our final answer must also be rounded to three significant digits.

v = + 38.8  m/s

Lesson Review

In this lesson, we established that the kinetic energy of an object is dictated by its mass and its velocity. You are now equipped to:

  • State the equation: K=12mv2
  • Calculate missing variables: You successfully rearranged the base equation entirely in variables before plugging in values to solve for velocity.
  • Understand the relationships: You learned that while changing mass linearly scales kinetic energy, changing velocity scales it exponentially.
